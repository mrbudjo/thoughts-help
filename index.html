Questions

Plain answers.

Is it private?

Yes. Your thoughts are yours alone. They are never shared or sold. Export or delete everything whenever you like.

Do I have to tag or organize anything?

No, just speak or type. Time and place are attached quietly. When you ask, it finds thoughts by meaning, not by exact words.

What happens when I say “forget”?

They’re forgotten for 30 days in case you change your mind, then are deleted for good.

Is it always listening?

Never. It records only while you hold the mic. Not in the background, not in meetings.

Are there limited AI credits?

No, one subscription covers unlimited usage on iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac. See Pricing for the plans.
