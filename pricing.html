Is there a free plan?

No. Thoughts is a paid app with no free tier and no ads. That’s how we keep it private and keep the AI unlimited.

How does billing work?

You subscribe through the App Store with your Apple ID. Apple bills you, and the subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. We never see your card details.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Thoughts. Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

What happens to my thoughts if I stop paying?

They stay yours. You can still export everything you’ve saved as CSV files, and reminders you already set still fire. You just can’t add, read or search thoughts in the app until you subscribe again.

Can I switch between weekly and yearly?

Yes, from the same Subscriptions screen. Moving to yearly takes effect straight away, with Apple crediting your unused weekly time. Moving to weekly takes effect when your year ends.
