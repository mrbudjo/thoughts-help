Thoughts uses artificial intelligence to turn your voice into text, to understand what you said, and to answer your questions. AI makes mistakes. In particular:

  • Transcripts can mishear words, names, numbers, dates and places, especially with background noise, accents, or more than one language.
  • Answers are generated from your own thoughts and may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. The app shows you the thoughts each answer came from so you can check.
  • Reminders and list changes made from plain words can be misunderstood. Always check the confirmation the app shows.

Answers are not advice. Nothing Thoughts says is medical, legal, financial, safety or other professional advice, even if the thought you saved was about those things. Don’t rely on the app for anything where a mistake could hurt you or someone else, and never for emergencies. Check important details against the original thought or another source before acting on them.

We’re always improving accuracy, but we can’t promise any answer, transcript or reminder is correct, complete or on time.
