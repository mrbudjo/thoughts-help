Privacy Policy

Your thoughts are yours.

This page says exactly what Thoughts collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to get it back or delete it.

Effective 10 September 2026 · So Easy Limited, New Zealand

The short version

  • We collect your name and email (from Sign in with Apple), the thoughts you record or type, and usage statistics (which you can turn off in Settings).
  • Location is opt-in. If you turn it on, we store where you were when you saved a thought. Saving a home location is a separate opt-in.
  • The app only records while you press the button. Never in the background.
  • Your thoughts are sent to AI providers to be transcribed, understood and answered. Your name and email are never sent to them.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for ads, and never share it with anyone except the providers listed below.
  • Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • You can access, correct, export or delete your data any time, in the app or by emailing privacy@thoughts.help.

What we collect

Thoughts is made by So Easy Limited, a company in New Zealand (“we”, “us”). We collect only what the app needs to remember things for you and answer your questions.

What we never do

  • Never record in the background. The microphone is on only while you are pressing or holding the record button, and the app shows you it’s recording.
  • Never sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • Never use your data for advertising, ours or anyone else’s. There are no ads in Thoughts.
  • Never send your name or email to AI providers. They receive your thoughts and questions, nothing that identifies you.
  • Never train our own AI models on your thoughts.
  • Built to answer only from what you said. If it’s not in your thoughts, the app says so.

How we use it

Everything we collect is used to provide Thoughts to you:

  • turning your voice into text;
  • understanding what you said so it can be found later by meaning, not just exact words;
  • answering your questions from your own thoughts, and showing which thoughts the answer came from;
  • keeping lists and reminders up to date, and delivering reminders on time;
  • keeping your account and subscription working across your devices;
  • answering support requests, fixing bugs, and improving the app.

Our staff may look at your data only when needed to answer a support request you’ve made, to investigate a problem, or to improve the app. We keep that access to as few people as possible.

Usage statistics tell us which features are used and where the app fails. They are linked to your account id, not your name or email, so we can see how the app is used across people, for example how many people who set a reminder also use lists. They never include the content of a thought. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

We will also disclose data if the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or to protect someone’s safety. If we ever sell or merge the business, your data would move with it under this same policy, and we’ll tell you.

Where it lives and how it’s protected

  • Your data is stored on servers in the United States.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between your devices, our servers and our providers.
  • Data is encrypted at rest on our servers.
  • Every account’s data is separated from every other account’s by database access rules, so one user can never read another’s thoughts.
  • Staff access is limited to what a support request or bug fix needs.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever learn of a breach that affects your data, we’ll tell you and the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

You can do most of this in the app, and all of it by emailing privacy@thoughts.help. We’ll answer within 20 working days, usually much sooner, and won’t charge for it.

  • Access. See everything you’ve saved in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Export. Download all your thoughts and lists as CSV files from Settings.
  • Correct. Edit any thought in the app, or tell it to fix something. Ask us to correct your name or email.
  • Delete. Forget any thought, or delete your whole account, from the app. Or ask us and we’ll do it.
  • Location. Turn location off any time in iOS Settings. Remove your home location in the app.
  • Usage statistics. Turn them off in Settings in the app. Nothing else changes.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in iOS Settings. Reminders you set will then only show inside the app.

These rights come from the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights your local privacy law gives you, including the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your local privacy regulator. We rely on your consent for location, on our legitimate interest in improving the app for usage statistics (which you can object to by turning them off), and on our contract with you for everything else the app needs to work.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first and we’ll try to fix it.

Minimum age

Thoughts is for people aged 13 or older (or the age of digital consent where you live, if that’s higher). We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe someone under the minimum age has an account, please email us. If we learn that we hold data from someone under the minimum age, we’ll delete it.

International transfers

We’re a New Zealand company and our servers and providers are in the United States. By using Thoughts, your data is transferred to and stored there. We only work with providers who commit to protecting your data to a standard comparable to New Zealand law, and each one is bound by contract to use it only for the purposes above.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything meaningful, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect. Small wording fixes will just be posted here with a new date.
